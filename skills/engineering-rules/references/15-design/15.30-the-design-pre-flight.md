# 15.30 [check] The design pre-flight

*Runs on every UI-bearing task, before Phase 4 ticks, and again inside the Phase 5
design-conformance check (12.5). A task with no UI surface records `pre-flight: no UI
surface` and does not run it.*

**Every box is ticked honestly, with the evidence named, or the page is not done.**

## Why a checklist, and why it is mechanical where it can be

A model under load skims a rule it has already read. Production tests of generated pages
found the same tells shipping again and again after the rules against them were in the
prompt: the em-dash, the section number, the fake product screenshot, the broken scroll
trigger. A rule the model can skip is not a rule. The rows below are the rules turned into
boxes, ordered so the cheap mechanical ones come first, and the design scout (15.29) decides
the ones a grep can decide so a tick is a copied result rather than an impression. A box
that cannot be honestly ticked sends the task back to Phase 3.

## Method

1. Open the composition plan (15.22) and the spec (15.3). Every box below is judged against
   them, never against taste.
2. Run the design scout over the touched scope with run-point id `pre-flight`; its rows
   answer the boxes marked *scout*.
3. Render the surface at 375, 768 and 1440px, in both themes where both ship, and with
   `prefers-reduced-motion` on. Screenshots are the evidence for the boxes marked *render*.
4. Walk the list. Tick with one line of evidence each. Any `no` is a Phase 3 loop-back with
   the box named as the reason.

## The list

**The read and the plan**
- [ ] The one-line design read was declared before code and matches the surface (15.20).
- [ ] The dials are explicit and reasoned from the read, none silently at baseline (15.20).
- [ ] The composition plan exists and the built page matches it section for section (15.22).
- [ ] A design system from 15.21 was chosen when the brief named one, or the aesthetic is
      labelled honestly; one system per project.
- [ ] On a redesign, the mode was detected, the audit ran, and nothing in "what never changes
      silently" moved without approval (15.28).
- [ ] `Rotated away from` was written and honoured (15.20).

**Copy** (15.23)
- [ ] Zero em-dashes and zero en-dash separators anywhere a user can read. *scout*
- [ ] The copy self-audit ran: every visible string re-read, broken and hallucinated
      strings rewritten, the count recorded.
- [ ] No filler verbs, no "Quietly in use at", no generic names, no slop brands, no lorem,
      no fake-perfect or fake-precise numbers. *scout*
- [ ] One copy register on the page; sentence-case headers; errors direct, success calm.
- [ ] Quotes at most three lines with name and role; typographic quote marks.
- [ ] One label per CTA intent across the page. *scout*
- [ ] No CTA wraps at desktop. *render*
- [ ] No section numbers, step numbers, poetic labels, micro-meta sentences, decoration
      strips, locale strips, version labels or scroll cues. *scout*

**Colour, theme and shape** (15.22, 15.26)
- [ ] One theme for the whole page; no mid-page inversion beyond one named device.
- [ ] One accent, used identically in every section; one grey family.
- [ ] On a premium-consumer brief, the palette is not the beige-brass-espresso family, or
      the spec argues it in writing; a different family from the previous project. *scout*
- [ ] No AI-purple gradient, no neon glow, no pure `#000000`, no gradient headline text,
      no mesh blobs. *scout*
- [ ] One corner-radius system, or a mixed one documented in the spec's Shapes section.
- [ ] Shadows tinted to the surface, one light direction.
- [ ] Both modes designed and tested; hierarchy parity and brand fidelity hold in the
      second mode. *render*

**Typography** (15.1, 15.23)
- [ ] The display face is not Inter, Roboto, Arial, a system stack or Space Grotesk, unless
      an official system mandates it. *scout*
- [ ] A serif display face, if any, has its reason written, is not Fraunces or Instrument
      Serif by reflex, and differs from the previous project's. *scout*
- [ ] Emphasis stays in one family; no serif word inside a sans headline.
- [ ] Every italic display word with a descender has line-height 1.1 or more and a bottom
      reserve. *scout*
- [ ] Body measure at 60-75 characters; figures in data are tabular.

**Hero** (15.22)
- [ ] Headline at most two lines, subtext at most 20 words and four lines, CTAs visible
      without scroll at 1440 and on a small laptop. *render*
- [ ] Top padding at most about 6rem; the font scale planned with the asset.
- [ ] At most four text elements; no tagline under the CTAs, no trust micro-strip, no
      pricing teaser, no bullets, no avatar row, no floating badges.
- [ ] The hero architecture was chosen against the read, with its reason in the plan.
- [ ] The hero has a real visual; no text-plus-blob, no div-built fake product UI.
- [ ] The logo wall sits under the hero, with real marks, logo only, both modes.

**Navigation and sections** (15.22)
- [ ] The nav renders on one line at 1024px and is at most 80px tall. *render*
- [ ] At least four layout families across eight sections; no family in adjacent sections;
      no family more than twice.
- [ ] No three consecutive image-and-text splits.
- [ ] Eyebrows at most ceil(sections / 3), the hero counting, never adjacent. *scout*
- [ ] No split-header pattern; no floating top-right sub-text.
- [ ] No cards inside cards inside cards; no giant rounded wrapper around every section.
- [ ] Section spacing even; density and background intensity vary across the page.
- [ ] Every bento has exactly as many cells as items, real visual variation in at least two
      cells, and rhythm rather than repetition.
- [ ] No three equal feature cards.
- [ ] Lists past five items use a different component; no hairline under every row; no
      data dump; no filled-track comparison bars. *scout, partly*

**Imagery and assets** (15.23)
- [ ] Real images in every slot the plan named: generated, real, or a seeded placeholder
      that is listed in the update log as needing a real asset. *scout*
- [ ] No hand-rolled decorative SVG, no hand-drawn icon paths, no div fake screenshots,
      no text-only page. *scout*
- [ ] Icons from one library, one stroke width; no emoji in the UI. *scout*
- [ ] No pills or labels overlaid on images; no photo-credit captions as decoration.
- [ ] Images sit in a frame system; text over images keeps a scrim or fade.

**Motion** (15.25)
- [ ] Every animation has its one-sentence reason in the plan.
- [ ] Motion claimed is motion shown: a dial above 4 moves; otherwise the dial was
      dropped to 3 and the page is cleanly static.
- [ ] At most one marquee. *scout*
- [ ] No window scroll listener, no scroll position in render state, no frame loop writing
      framework state. *scout*
- [ ] Only `transform` and `opacity` animate; springs or decelerating curves on
      interactive elements. *scout*
- [ ] Sticky stacks and horizontal pans follow the canonical skeletons (start at the
      viewport top, pinned, scrubbed, cleaned up).
- [ ] Everything above dial 3 collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`. *scout, render*
- [ ] Animated components are isolated leaves with cleanup; one engine per tree.

**States, forms and access** (15.22, 15.26)
- [ ] Every button's label passes AA against its fill; ghost buttons over photos carry a
      scrim or stroke. *scout, partly*
- [ ] Every form string passes AA against its section; labels above, errors below, no
      placeholder-as-label; client-side validation present.
- [ ] Loading, empty and error states exist for every component that can be in them.
- [ ] Hover, press, focus and disabled states exist; a visible focus ring everywhere.
- [ ] No dead links; the current page is indicated.
- [ ] Contrast numbers computed for every changed pair, not asserted (15.3, 12.5).

**Mobile and performance** (15.26)
- [ ] Every multi-column layout declares its under-768 collapse; single column, full width,
      no horizontal scroll at 375. *scout, render*
- [ ] `min-height: 100dvh`, never `h-screen` or `100vh`. *scout*
- [ ] Tap targets at least 44px; body text at least 14px on a phone. *render*
- [ ] No arbitrary z-index; the project's scale is used. *scout*
- [ ] Grain and blur only on fixed or sticky layers. *scout*
- [ ] Lighthouse run: LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1, with the numbers
      pasted.
- [ ] Every dependency imported exists in the manifest.

**Chrome and completeness** (15.23)
- [ ] Favicon, title, description, social tags, skip link, 404, legal links, a way back.
- [ ] Semantic landmarks; descriptive alt on every meaningful image. *scout, partly*
- [ ] Every section and screen the request asked for is built in full; no placeholder
      comments, no "similar to above", the deliverable count matches the plan. *scout*
- [ ] The update log names every placeholder slot and every asset still needed.

## Verification

   row or its clean coverage line? (yes / no)
2. Are the three widths, both themes and the reduced-motion run evidenced by screenshots?
   (yes / no)
3. Does every ticked box carry one line of evidence rather than a bare tick? (yes / no)
4. Was every `no` turned into a Phase 3 loop-back with the box named? (yes / no)
